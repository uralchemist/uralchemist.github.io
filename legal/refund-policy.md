## When we refund
Sales are final once a file has been downloaded, except in these cases:

- **Faulty file:** the file is damaged or will not open.
- **Not as described:** the file does not match its product description.
- **Duplicate purchase:** you bought the same product twice by mistake.

For a faulty or not-as-described file, contact us within 14 days of purchase. We will send a working replacement or give you a full refund. Duplicate purchases are refunded on request.

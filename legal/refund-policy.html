Digital products and the right of withdrawal

All products are digital files that are made available immediately after payment. By completing checkout and starting the download, you agree to immediate delivery and acknowledge that you lose your right of withdrawal once the download has begun. Before the download starts, you may cancel and receive a full refund.

When we refund

Sales are final once a file has been downloaded, except in these cases:

  • Faulty file: the file is damaged or will not open.
  • Not as described: the file does not match its product description.
  • Duplicate purchase: you bought the same product twice by mistake.

For a faulty or not-as-described file, contact us within 14 days of purchase. We will send a working replacement or give you a full refund. Duplicate purchases are refunded on request.

How to request a refund

Email youareanalchemist@gmail.com with your order number (from your Lemon Squeezy receipt) and a short description of the problem. We aim to reply within 3 business days. Approved refunds are issued through Lemon Squeezy to your original payment method; banks can take up to 10 days to show them.

This policy does not limit any rights you have under the consumer law of your country, including rights for digital content that is faulty or not as described.

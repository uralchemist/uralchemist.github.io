Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy explains how Asten Family Marketing Services ("we") handles personal data in connection with the website https://uralchemist.github.io/ and our products sold in the Magic Store on Lemon Squeezy.

Controller and contact

Asten Family Marketing Services, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Email: youareanalchemist@gmail.com

What this website collects

The website is a static page. It has no forms, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising trackers and sets no cookies of its own.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you visit, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes, as described at https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/about-github-pages. GitHub processes this data under its own privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Purchases

When you buy a product, checkout is handled by Lemon Squeezy, the merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy collects your name, email address, billing country and payment details, and is responsible for that processing under its privacy policy: https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/privacy. We do not receive your full card details.

Lemon Squeezy shares order data with us (such as your name, email address, country, product and order number) so that we can provide downloads, support and refunds. We use it only for these purposes and to meet legal and accounting obligations. Legal basis (EU/UK GDPR): performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) and legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).

Emails you send us

If you email us, we use your email address and message to reply. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering enquiries and, where relevant, performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b), (f) GDPR).

Marketing

We do not send marketing emails unless you have opted in. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Retention

We keep order and support records for as long as needed to provide support and to meet tax and accounting requirements, and support emails no longer than necessary to resolve your request.

International transfers

We are based in the UAE, and our service providers (Lemon Squeezy, GitHub, Google for email) may process data in the United States and other countries. Where EU/UK data is transferred, these providers rely on their own transfer safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses).

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the EU/UK GDPR and the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to processing, and to data portability. To use these rights, email youareanalchemist@gmail.com. If you are in the EU/UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Our products may be used by children, but purchases must be made by an adult. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
